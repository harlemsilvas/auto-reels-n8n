Privacy Policy

Revision prepared on October 13, 2026

1. Responsible party and scope

This policy describes how SocialBot processes data as an internal dashboard of HRM MOTOS PECAS E ACESSORIOS LTDA for its own professional accounts. It covers people who communicate with those accounts on Instagram and authorized internal dashboard users.

SocialBot supports receiving and replying to individual messages in its Inbox, organizing, scheduling and publishing content, and retrieving metrics through official Meta APIs. Dashboard access uses separate authentication; connecting Meta accounts uses Facebook Login.

2. Data processed

Connected accounts: names and identifiers of Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages, access tokens, available authorization information and expiry dates. Tokens are used by the server to operate the integration.

Conversations: sender and recipient identifiers, names and usernames when available, text, dates, read status, attachment information and events received from Meta. Events may contain copies of message data.

Sends and images: text and identifiers of send attempts, available delivery status and confirmation, images sent through the dashboard and references to received attachments. The send-attempt record helps prevent resending when delivery is uncertain.

Content and operations: media files, captions, hashtags, schedules, campaign templates, text suggestions, metrics and action history. For internal users, we also process usernames, names, optional email addresses, access roles and password hashes.

Security: session information, IP addresses, browser information, access dates, audit records and technical logs. Backups may contain data from these categories.

3. Purposes

We use data to provide individual support for conversations initiated on Instagram, display history and attachments to authorized staff, and record replies. The Inbox is not intended for unsolicited bulk messaging.

We also use data to authorize integrations, manage publications and metrics, assist content preparation, authenticate internal users, prevent duplicates and incidents, maintain the service and assess requests concerning data.

Processing should be limited to what is necessary for these purposes and applicable permissions. This policy does not represent advanced access or approval of the app by Meta.

4. Sharing and services used

Meta provides events, account and profile information, and receives reply, publication and metrics requests according to the feature used. Processing by Meta itself is also subject to its own policies.

The application is hosted on a Hostinger VPS. Backups currently reside on the same VPS. Infrastructure providers and authorized people may have access needed for operations. Email requests are received in HRM’s sales mailbox.

The Reels publication workflow uses n8n automation. When an operator requests text suggestions through Google Gemini, product and campaign information and operator-entered fields may be sent to that service. The current workflow does not automatically forward DMs to Gemini.

We do not sell or rent personal data. Any disclosure in response to legal requests depends on assessing their validity and necessary scope. Further information about providers, location and access can be requested from the responsible party.

5. Storage and protection

The dashboard restricts access through authentication, sessions and permissions. Public communications use HTTPS. Images sent through the Inbox are held in private storage and made available to Meta through temporary delivery links.

Integration tokens are kept on the server and are not displayed in the account listing. Protection applied to AI credentials does not mean every record, token or backup is encrypted at rest. We do not promise absolute security.

The responsible party should limit access and review the handling of logs, credentials and backups.

6. Retention and backups

Retention should be limited to the time necessary for the stated purposes and applicable obligations. Category-specific periods and their operational enforcement are under review; currently there is no general automatic expiry routine for DMs, send-attempt records, files, logs and backups.

Expiry of a session, cache or media link does not delete history or backups. Uncertain delivery attempts require review before records are removed, to avoid unintended resends.

Files may be shared across campaigns and publications. Deletion requests must consider these references, copies in events and send records, and backups. We do not assume that backup rotation or revoking access on Meta already removes all copies.

7. Rights and deletion requests

People whose data is processed may request information, access, correction or deletion and raise questions through the contact below. This channel is also available to people without dashboard access.

To request deletion, identify the Instagram account involved and provide the minimum information needed to locate the interaction. Do not send passwords, tokens, full conversation copies or documents unnecessarily. The responsible party may request proportionate information to verify the legitimacy and scope of the request.

The responsible party personally handles requests and will explain their handling and any limitations related to retention obligations or backups. At this stage, no fixed completion deadline is announced and no automated deletion callback is available.

The account owner can revoke permissions through Meta’s controls. Revoking access, disabling a dashboard account or hiding a conversation does not prove deletion of history. To request removal of data held by SocialBot, use the contact.

8. Cookies and browser storage

The dashboard uses a session cookie, temporary storage for operation protection and recovery of drafts or uncertain sends, and a local language preference. These support authentication, security and continuity of support.

These static policy pages require no login and use no tracking scripts. The language selector only navigates between the Portuguese and English versions.
