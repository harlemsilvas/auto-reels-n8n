Data deletion

Revision prepared on October 13, 2026

You can request deletion of personal data processed by SocialBot, including if you do not have dashboard access. The channel is personally handled by the responsible party of HRM MOTOS PECAS E ACESSORIOS LTDA.

How to request deletion

  1. Email exclusao@hrmmotos.com.br with the subject “SocialBot — data deletion request”.
  2. Provide your Instagram username and the HRM account you communicated with or whose integration you want to end. If needed, provide an approximate date range to help locate the interaction.
  3. Describe which data you want deleted. Do not send passwords, tokens, documents or complete copies of messages unnecessarily.
  4. Wait for the responsible party’s response. If additional information is needed to verify your relationship to the data and the scope of the request, proportionate information will be requested.

Privacy contact

exclusao@hrmmotos.com.br is an alias of vendas@hrmmotos.com.br. Requests are handled by HRM’s responsible party. You do not need to create a SocialBot account or provide access to your Meta account.

Scope of the request

The request will be assessed based on your relationship to the data, including message history, attachments, copies of events and send records held by SocialBot. The responsible party will explain its handling and any retention or backup limitations.

At this stage, no fixed completion deadline or automatic deletion confirmation is announced. Sending the email requests assistance; it does not automatically erase data.

The request concerns data held by SocialBot. Deleting content on Meta itself is handled through Instagram or Facebook controls and policies.

Revoking Meta permissions

The account owner can revoke permissions through Meta’s controls for connected apps or integrations. This may interrupt future access but does not prove removal of history already stored by SocialBot. Hiding conversations or disabling accounts in the dashboard does not delete that data either.
